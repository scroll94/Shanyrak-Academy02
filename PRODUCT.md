# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS in one `index.html`, published with GitHub Pages from `scroll94/Shanyrak-Academy02` (inferred from the user's request to publish on GitHub). Libraries from CDN: Three.js, GSAP, Lenis.

## Users
Teenagers 14–18 in Kazakhstan choosing where to learn programming and AI, and their parents, who decide and pay. They arrive from a shared link, mostly on phones.

## Product Purpose
Шаңырақ Code is an online school of AI and programming. The site exists to get a teenager (or a parent) to sign up for a trial lesson.

## Positioning
Seven connected directions under one roof: games, Roblox, chat-bots, 3D websites, mobile apps, 3D modelling, and understanding and using AI honestly. Every module ends with a finished project for the student's portfolio.

## Operating Context
Lessons are online. A trial lesson comes first, then basics, then the student's own project, then a demo day.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Sign-up form has no backend: it assembles the application text for the visitor to send to the teacher.
- Undecided, must not be invented: prices, schedule, lesson length, group size, teacher bio, contacts, student results.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Шаңырақ Code (the shanyrak is the crown of the yurt).
- Kazakh visual world requested by the owner: ornaments (қошқар мүйіз), yurt, berkut (golden eagle), shanyrak; bright animations and 3D are explicitly wanted.
- Language: Russian with Kazakh words.

## Evidence on Hand
No own photographs, testimonials, numbers or press. Archive pictures with credits in `assets/credits.json`. Nothing may be fabricated.

## Product Principles
1. AI is a partner, not a cheat: honesty rules are part of the offer.
2. Projects over theory: every module ends with a real thing.
3. Speak to the teenager first, answer the parent second.
